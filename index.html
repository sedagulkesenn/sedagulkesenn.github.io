Our Services

Based in Luxembourg, Lopeseco specialises in interior design, bespoke furniture and custom carpentry. From kitchens and dressing rooms to complete home renovations, we create functional, personalised spaces with attention to every detail.

We design spaces that inspire.

Based in Luxembourg, our design studio brings together architecture, design and craftsmanship to create interiors that are both functional and timeless.

From custom-made furniture and tailor-designed kitchens to kids’ rooms, bathrooms, dressing areas and smart storage solutions, we bring creative ideas to life with precision and care. We also design and install wood floors, interior doors and carry out complete renovations from start to finish. Our work spans private residences as well as business projects, always tailored to the needs and lifestyle of our clients.
