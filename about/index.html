Lopeseco Design Studio, led by André Lopeseco, specializes in custom furniture, carpentry and interior design in Luxembourg. We design and create bespoke kitchens, wardrobes, dressing rooms, bathrooms and complete interior solutions tailored to each client.

Our office

Located in Bivange, Luxembourg, our team of professional designers and carpenters welcomes you to discuss your ideas, needs and budget. Together, we find the right solutions, combining thoughtful design, functionality and expert craftsmanship. From custom kitchens and dressing rooms to TV units, storage solutions, bathroom furniture and hallway furniture, we create tailored interiors that suit your space and lifestyle.
